# Community conduct

trust-cg welcomes technical disagreement and careful scrutiny. Participants in
the project's repositories and communication channels are expected to be
respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.
Good-faith bug reports, benchmark losses, proof gaps, and corrections to project
claims are welcome and must not be suppressed for being inconvenient.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately to `andrewyates.name@gmail.com`. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed
and routed away from the subject of the report when possible.
